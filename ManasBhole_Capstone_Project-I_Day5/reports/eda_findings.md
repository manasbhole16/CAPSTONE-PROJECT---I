# 10 Key EDA Findings — Bluestock MF Analytics

- F1 (→ Chart 01): 2023 was a strong bull-run year — average NAV across all schemes grew ~14.3% year-on-year, followed by a correction in late 2024.

- F2 (→ Chart 03): Monthly SIP inflow reached an all-time high of ₹31,002 Cr in Dec 2025, demonstrating strong retail investor conviction.

- F3 (→ Chart 10): Total folios grew from 13.26 Cr to 26.12 Cr (+97.0%), reflecting significant new investor participation.

- F4 (→ Chart 07): Male investors account for ~67% of transactions; female investors are ~33%, indicating gender gap in MF participation.

- F5 (→ Chart 09): T30 cities contribute ~66% of transactions, while B30 cities contribute ~34%, suggesting growing penetration in Tier-2/3 towns.

- F6 (→ Chart 13): Direct plans have a mean expense ratio of 0.78% vs 1.35% for Regular plans — savers in Direct plans retain more returns.

- F7 (→ Chart 12): 'Banking' is the most heavily weighted sector in equity funds with an aggregate weight of 652.3%, indicating sector concentration risk.

- F8 (→ Chart 11): The fund with the highest 3-year return is 'SBI Small Cap Fund - Regular Plan - Grow' at 23.4%, significantly above its benchmark (22.2%).

- F9 (→ Chart 05): The '26-35' age group is the largest investor cohort (~41.1%), suggesting MFs are most popular among working-age adults.

- F10 (→ Chart 11): NAV return correlations among large-cap funds are high (>0.85), indicating limited diversification benefit within the same category — investors should diversify across categories.
